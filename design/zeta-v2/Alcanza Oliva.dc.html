3Oliva con jerarquía clara · acciones ≠ estados ≠ datos

This builds on 2c with one rule: black means you can tap it, color means how you're doing, gray is data. The earlier versions mixed these up in three ways, and this fixes each one. Verdicts and buttons were both colored pills: now a verdict is a round pill with a dot, and a button is a black rectangle. The chips on a row looked like answers: now they're small tags with a gray border. The dark CTA cards looked like states: now they're neutral cards with a black button. Olive now appears only in Vas bien.
