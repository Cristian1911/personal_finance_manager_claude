1Historial · las dos direcciones iniciales

Working name Alcanza, from “¿me alcanza?”. Both directions show the same data and the same rules: one number, one verdict, the math behind it, and the next payments. 1a Sereno is editorial: a serif number, lines instead of cards, color only in the verdict dot. 1b Clara is bold and friendly: the verdict colors the whole card, with a heavy grotesque and rounded cards. I went with 1b because you read the state at a glance, before the number.
