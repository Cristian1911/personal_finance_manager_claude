Zeta v2 · Planning session 1 of 6

How Zeta v2 works

This session covers where each part of Zeta runs, how a purchase gets from your bank to your Disponible, and what happens when the phone is offline or the app is closed. It ends with four decisions for you. We'll take them one at a time in the chat.

The four places things happen

Zeta v2 is one app on your phone backed by one server and one database. There's no separate web product anymore. The web app stays only as the backend plus a small page for bulk PDF import.

The one rule

The server owns the truth. The phone keeps a copy. Anything that creates money data or links it to other data (a new purchase, a merged duplicate, a paid bill, a split) is decided on the server, by one pipeline. The phone shows it first, then confirms it.

Why: the old app let the phone write rows straight into the database. So the phone had to repeat every side effect the web did (update the account balance, mark the bill paid, check for duplicates). It missed some, and the 2026-06-25 audit found 8 data bugs caused by exactly that. One pipeline in one place removes that whole class of bug.

phone keeps and does

  • A copy of your recent data (proposed: last 3 cycles)
  • Calculates Disponible and the verdict, instantly and offline
  • Reads bank notifications and matches them against templates
  • An outbox of captures waiting to be sent
  • Bill reminders scheduled on the phone

server keeps and does

  • All your data, encrypted; the source of truth
  • The ingest pipeline: duplicates, merchant, category, bills, balances
  • Gmail reading, PDF parsing, merchant naming
  • The weekly digest and payday pushes
  • The signed pack of bank notification templates

Decisions for this session

Each decision has a recommendation. We'll go through them one at a time in the chat.

S1-1

Should the phone keep its own copy of your data?

RECOMMENDEDA · Local copyKeep the existing sync engine: it already handles single-flight runs, resets and retries. Sync 13 tables instead of 21. About 300 lines of change.
B · Online-firstSimpler to reason about, but it means rewriting every read (about 2,000 lines) and the number isn't there without signal. We'd still need a local queue for captures.
S1-2

Where do new movements get decided?

RECOMMENDEDOne server doorEvery capture (notification, Gmail, PDF, manual, voice) goes through /api/ingest. We build it by pulling the pipeline out of today's email webhook. The phone shows the row immediately as "pendiente".
Phone decides, then syncsWorks fully offline, including duplicate checks and bill matching. But it repeats the pipeline on the phone, which is the cause of today's parity bugs.
S1-3

How much history lives on the phone?

This matters because of D2 (browsing past cycles). Today the phone keeps only this month and last month of movements.

RECOMMENDEDLast 3 cycles + saved cycle resultsThe last 3 cycles of movements stay on the phone. Each closed cycle's result (the four lines plus the category totals) is saved as a small record and synced, so past cycles open instantly. Movements older than that load from the server when you open them.
EverythingAll movements on the phone. Simple, but the first sync is slow for heavy users (one user has 3,000+ movements) and the phone does more work on every sync.
S1-4

What does the web companion do?

RECOMMENDEDPDF bulk import + accounts onlyUpload several statements at once and manage accounts from a computer. The old web dashboard stays reachable until v2 ships, then gets retired.
Also a read-only DisponibleUseful on a laptop, but it's a second surface to keep correct, the parity tax again.
