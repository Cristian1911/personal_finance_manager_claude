How Disponible is calculated

The one number: how much you can still spend until your next payday. This page covers how each kind of movement affects it, with real numbers for cards, cuotas, loans, bills, Te deben and Tú debes, and when the verdict changes. The same function runs on the phone and the server. Four decisions at the end.

The formula

Disponible = Llega este ciclo − Por pagar − Ahorro − Ya salió (± ajustes) Por día = Disponible ÷ días que faltan (incluye hoy), rounded down to $100

Llega este ciclo

Expected salary, replaced by the real amount once it arrives. Plus other income you received and repayments ("Te pagaron").

Por pagar

Bills, loan cuotas, the unreserved part of card bills, and money you owe people (Tú debes) that are due before next payday and not paid yet.

Ahorro

What you set aside this cycle. It's 0 unless you choose an amount.

Ya salió

Money that left this cycle: purchases (card ones included, at the moment you buy), cash, money lent, paid bills. Transfers between your accounts and card payments don't count.

What is a cycle

A cycle runs from a payday to the day before the next one. Paid on the 15th and 30th means two cycles a month: 15–29 and 30–14. If a payday falls on a weekend or holiday, the salary is expected the business day before. The cycle starts when the money actually arrives, or on the expected date with a "~" if it hasn't. With irregular income, a cycle is simply the calendar month.

The pattern: money that is already promised (bills, cuotas, debts) is subtracted before you pay it. So paying it never surprises your number, and only new, unplanned money moves it.

The verdict: Vas bien, Cuidado, Te pasaste

One function, used for Disponible, límites and trips. It compares today's daily allowance with the one at the start of the cycle.

No flicker: the verdict gets worse immediately, but only improves after the better state has held for 24 hours. A refund that briefly lifts you back to "Vas bien" doesn't make the pill jump back and forth.

How we know it's right

The whole calculation is one pure function, computeDisponible, in @zeta/shared. It gets no database and no dates of its own; everything comes in as input. Every row of the tables above becomes an automated test, written before the code: 19 cases from the design docs plus the new ones from your decisions (partial bills, pay-off, Tú debes, cycle summaries). The phone and the server both run this exact function.

Decisions for this session

S3-1

How does an ATM withdrawal count?

You take $200.000 from the ATM. Later you might log "almuerzo $30.000 en efectivo", or not.

RECOMMENDEDCounts at withdrawal; logging cash only re-labels itDisponible drops $200.000 when you withdraw (category "Efectivo y otros"). Logging a cash lunch later moves $30.000 from "Efectivo" to "Restaurantes" without touching Disponible. The number is right whether or not you log cash.
Cash is a wallet accountThe withdrawal is a transfer to "Efectivo" (no change), and each cash expense lowers Disponible. More precise per category, but if you don't log every cash expense, Disponible stays too high.
S3-2

Big card purchases before you say how many cuotas

RECOMMENDED≥ $300.000 counts in full until answeredConservative: the number is never too optimistic. A "¿A cuántas cuotas?" question appears on the row and in Revisar. Below $300.000, 1 cuota is assumed. You can change the threshold in Ajustes.
Assume the usual (e.g. 1 cuota) silentlyNo question, but a 12-cuota phone would wrongly eat a whole cycle until the PDF arrives.
S3-3

What happens to money left over at the end of a cycle?

RECOMMENDEDAsk; if you don't answer, it stays available"Te sobraron $85.000 · ¿A ahorro o lo dejas?" If you ignore it, a line "Te sobró +$85.000" joins the new cycle.
Ask; if you don't answer, it goes to savingsEncourages saving, but it silently lowers the new cycle's number.
S3-4

How strict is "Cuidado"?

RECOMMENDED85%, fixed, 24 h to improveAs designed. One threshold everywhere keeps the words meaning the same thing on every screen. We tune it once with real data after launch.
Let the user choose (relaxed / normal / strict)Personal, but it lets the same state mean different things for different people, and it adds a setting few will understand.
