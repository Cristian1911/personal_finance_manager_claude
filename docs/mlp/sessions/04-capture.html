How movements get into Zeta

Five ways a movement arrives: Android bank notifications, bank emails, PDF statements, statements sent by email, and you. This page shows where each one enters, how notifications are read and learned, what happens when each path fails, and what leaves your phone. Five decisions at the end.

Five doors, one result

Reading a bank notification, step by step

Everything below runs on the phone. Nothing from apps you didn't choose is ever stored, logged or sent.

How Zeta learns notifications, and stays current

Templates you train

  • An unknown notice becomes a training card. You tap the amount, merchant and card in the text, and choose Salió / Entró / No es un movimiento.
  • Zeta turns your taps into a template: fixed words plus slots ({monto}, {comercio}, {tarjeta4}).
  • It tests the template against the last 20 notices from that bank: "También reconoce 3 avisos anteriores".
  • A template you trained auto-captures only after 2 matches you confirmed. Until then its matches arrive as prefilled Revisar cards.

Templates Zeta ships

  • A pack of templates per bank, signed by the server and verified by the app, downloaded without an app release.
  • v1 banks: Bancolombia, Nu, Nequi, Davivienda, Banco de Bogotá, Falabella, Lulo. Built from ~30 real notices per bank (yours plus volunteers').
  • Wording change: 3 partial matches from one bank within 48 h raise a flag to us and a banner for you ("Bancolombia cambió sus avisos…"). The fixed pack resolves pending cards on its own.
  • Your notices never feed the pack unless you opt in (v1.1, amounts and names removed).

Decisions for this session

S4-1

iPhone: how do bank emails reach Zeta?

DECIDED Both. Forwarding address with guided setup at launch; Google verification (CASA) starts now; "Conectar Gmail" for up to 100 testers meanwhile, for everyone once approved.

S4-2

One template engine for notifications and emails?

DECIDED One template engine for notifications and bank emails (signed pack, training, wording-change detection). The Bancolombia email parser becomes the first templates, proven identical by replaying the saved email corpus.

S4-3

Upload the raw text of bank notices?

DECIDED Upload raw notice text, encrypted, with an off switch (only from banks you chose), for Origen on any device and for diagnosing misreads.

S4-4

AI in capture, given that the app works offline

DECIDED Deterministic first; AI only refines, online. One small model (Claude Haiku) for naming new merchants and hard voice text, replacing today's Gemini call. Jev gatekeeper: v1.1 after its eval.

S4-5

Balance notices ("tu saldo disponible es…")

DECIDED Balance notices check, never adjust on their own. Equal → silent "✓ cuadrado"; different → a Revisar card offering an adjustment.

Next: session 5, the visual direction. It covers fixing Oliva's contrast and hierarchy in light and dark, and timeline variations.
