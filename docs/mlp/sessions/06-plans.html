Plans and payments

What stays free, what becomes Zeta Plus, how paying works on iPhone and Android (including offline), what the server enforces, and when billing ships. Screenshots are the first paid candidate (S4-7). Six decisions at the end.

The principle

The promise is free; the extras that cost us or go beyond it are Plus. Knowing how much you can spend, having movements arrive on their own, bills, Te deben, working offline and your data are never behind a paywall. Plus pays for what has a real cost per use (AI reading screenshots) or is a nice extra for people who already love the app.

How paying works

Rules for the paywall

  • It appears only when you tap a Plus feature. No pop-ups on launch, no nagging, no countdowns.
  • It shows exactly what you get and the price per month and per year, in pesos.
  • "Restaurar compras" in Ajustes; cancel from the store, one link away.
  • If Plus ends, your data stays: trips become read-only, and the theme goes back to Oliva.

What it adds to the build

  • RevenueCat SDK in the app; its user id = your Zeta user id.
  • A webhook route on the server and one new table, entitlements (the 16th new table).
  • Products set up in App Store Connect and Google Play Console, with prices in COP.
  • A paywall screen and "Tu plan" in Ajustes (for Claude Design, S5-3).
