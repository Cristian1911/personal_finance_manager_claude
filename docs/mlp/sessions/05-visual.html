Why Oliva feels flat

The words aren't the problem. The shapes are: cards melt into the background, chip borders are too faint to read as "tappable", and "Vas bien" is a tint so pale it looks like an ordinary card. Everything sits in the same beige, so nothing leads.

Three directions

The timeline (S3-T): "Tu flujo", interactive

Built on the flow chart Zeta has today (plan-flow-chart.tsx): the balance line, daily bars around $0, and "Hoy". What changes: it follows your pay cycles instead of the calendar month, it projects the rest of the cycle and the next one (dashed line, outlined bars for bills still to pay), and you can touch it. Try it: drag across the chart to read the balance on any day, tap a day to see its movements and bills, and switch cycles. The theme toggles show it in both themes.

Tu flujo (full screen) · drag · tap · swipe

What it shows

  • Line: money in your counted accounts at the end of each day. Solid up to today, dashed after (projected with your usual daily spending).
  • Bars up: money in (salary, repayments).
  • Bars down: money out. Solid = already happened. Outlined = a bill or payment still to come. Gray = estimated daily spending.
  • Marker: the lowest point ahead, i.e. "will I make it?".
  • Touch: drag to read any day's balance; tap to list that day's items (each opens its detail sheet in the app).
  • Cycles: Pasado / Este / Próximo, or swipe. Totals under the chart follow the selected cycle.
  • Edges: 2 days before and after the cycle, shaded, so you see how it connects (e.g. the next salary arriving).
  • Below $0: the line and the area under $0 turn red, the day you'd run out is named, and a card says how much less to spend per day.
  • A big payment next cycle: an amber card on the current cycle warns early ("El próximo ciclo no alcanza…") with "Apartar" (S3-7).
  • How you get here: a "Tu flujo" row on Inicio with a small line and one sentence ("Llegas al 29 con ≈ $86.100", or the warning); tapping it opens this full screen. Every bill in Pagos also has "Ver en tu flujo".

One change from today's chart: everyday spending bars are neutral, not red. Red is reserved for "Te pasaste" (the hierarchy rule), so it keeps its meaning.

Inicio: one number on top, cards you choose below

Today's dashboard repeats the same facts in several widgets. v2 keeps the widget idea, planned from scratch, but with one rule: only the top block answers "¿cómo voy?". Every card below answers a different question, shows one line when collapsed, and expands in place (the "toca para ver" pattern from today's Plan hero). "Ver todo ›" opens the full screen. Try it: tap a card to expand it; "Editar Inicio" adds, removes and orders cards.

Planned from scratch; today's widget catalog isn't carried over. Rules for every card: one line when collapsed, details when expanded, "Ver todo ›" for the full screen. No two cards answer the same question. Only Límites and Viaje show a verdict, and it comes from the same verdict function as the top block.

Where every feature lives

  • Deudas personales (Te deben / Tú debes): its Inicio card → full screen. Also from any movement: "Fue compartido".
  • Deudas con bancos (tarjetas, créditos): Pagos › Deudas tab; its Inicio card is optional.
  • Modos / Viajes (v1.1): created from "+"; its card appears on Inicio while a trip is upcoming or on.
  • Límites: its card once limits exist (suggested from week 3).
  • Everything else: Movimientos and Revisar tabs; Ajustes from the avatar.

Default for a new user: Tu flujo, Próximos pagos, Te deben. Contextual cards (weekly summary, statement due, a quiet source, new cycle) appear on their own and can't be pinned.
