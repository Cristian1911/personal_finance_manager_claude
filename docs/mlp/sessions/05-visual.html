Why Oliva feels flat

The words aren't the problem. The shapes are: cards melt into the background, chip borders are too faint to read as "tappable", and "Vas bien" is a tint so pale it looks like an ordinary card. Everything sits in the same beige, so nothing leads.

Three directions

The timeline (S3-T): "Tu flujo", interactive

Built on the flow chart Zeta has today (plan-flow-chart.tsx): the balance line, daily bars around $0, and "Hoy". What changes: it follows your pay cycles instead of the calendar month, it projects the rest of the cycle and the next one (dashed line, outlined bars for bills still to pay), and you can touch it. Try it: drag across the chart to read the balance on any day, tap a day to see its movements and bills, and switch cycles. The theme toggles show it in both themes.

What it shows

  • Line: money in your counted accounts at the end of each day. Solid up to today, dashed after (projected with your usual daily spending).
  • Bars up: money in (salary, repayments).
  • Bars down: money out. Solid = already happened. Outlined = a bill or payment still to come. Gray = estimated daily spending.
  • Marker: the lowest point ahead, i.e. "will I make it?".
  • Touch: drag to read any day's balance; tap to list that day's items (each opens its detail sheet in the app).
  • Cycles: Pasado / Este / Próximo, or swipe. Totals under the chart follow the selected cycle.

One change from today's chart: everyday spending bars are neutral, not red. Red is reserved for "Te pasaste" (the hierarchy rule), so it keeps its meaning.
