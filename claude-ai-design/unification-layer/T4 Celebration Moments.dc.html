4a Zeta · unification layer · T4

Celebration moments

The app is fluent at danger and mute at achievement. The fix is not confetti — it is letting brass touch a number for the first time. Today brass means “you can act”; in a celebration it briefly means “you did.” One earned moment per event, built from the materials the app already owns: the eyebrow, the big number, the T1 verdict, the T2 ring, the T3 curve. Quiet, typographic, over in under a second. All three demos are live — use the Tweaks buttons to replay them.

a · el momento — anatomía
TRIGGER Earned events only: a debt reaches zero, a month closes under plan, the last pending recurrente clears. Never for opening the app, never for streaks, never twice for the same event. BRASS The hero number turns brass. This is the entire spectacle — the one color the user has learned to read as “valuable” lands on their result. It holds while the moment is on screen, then the state returns to normal ink white. EYEBROW The eyebrow re-labels with warmth in the same 10px uppercase voice: “DEUDA SALDADA”, “MES CERRADO BAJO PLAN”, “TODO PAGADO”. The micro-label system carries the emotion; no new type style. VERDICT The T1 chip stays “Vas bien” — celebrations do not add a fifth state. The detail line names the achievement in one sentence, tuteo, no exclamation. MOTION T3 budget only: the ring sweeps closed once (600ms, the single exception to 200ms, earned), text rises on the 120ms enter. No particles, no scale, no shimmer. RECORD The moment leaves a residue: a compact “logro” row in Actividad reciente, so the achievement is findable later without replaying it.
Regla de restricción One celebration per screen visit, max. If two events land together (last cuota paid + month under plan), the larger one speaks and the smaller becomes its detail line. Brass-on-number is reserved for these moments — it never appears in ordinary state, which is what keeps it worth something.
b · los tres eventos
Deuda saldada A balance hits zero. The T2 ring sweeps to full, the number lands as $0 in brass, the detail totals the journey: “Pagaste $8.400.000 en 14 meses.” The debt row moves to a “Saldadas” group instead of vanishing.
Mes cerrado bajo plan On the first visit after month close, the Plan hero shows the closed month: what stayed unspent, in brass, with the margin as detail. One visit only; afterwards the month renders in normal history form.
Todo pagado The last pending recurrente clears and the “Por resolver” inbox empties. The inbox does not disappear — it becomes the calm proof: “11 de 11 pagados”. The smallest of the three; a state, not a scene.

Why brass and not green: income green marks money that arrived; it is routine. Brass is the brand’s own metal and appears on nothing passive — borrowing it for one beat is the strongest signal the system can make without a new hue.
