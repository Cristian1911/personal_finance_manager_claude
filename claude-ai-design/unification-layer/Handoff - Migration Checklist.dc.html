7a Zeta · unification layer · handoff

Migration checklist

The unification layer in one page. Four new primitives, one theme object, six copy rules — no rebrand, no new tokens, no new hues. Each item links its territory board; sequence is by leverage. Definition of done, globally: every screen answers ¿voy bien? in the hero slot without explanation.

a · qué se construye — 4 primitivas + 1 tema
<Verdict /> T1 →
Pill chip + detail line. Props: state (vas-bien | cerca | te-pasaste | atencion), delta?, detail?, compact?. Colors: income / alert / debt tokens, 8% tint + 20% border. Fixed hero slot: eyebrow → number → verdict → detail → meta. One full-volume verdict per screen.
<Disclosure /> T3 →
Grid-rows 0fr→1fr 160ms, caret 180°, content enter 120ms. Label “Ver + qué” / “Ocultar …”. Tokens: --motion-ease: cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1), --motion-1/2/3: 120/160/200ms. Dim only on focus-leaving overlays.
<MonthControl /> T5 →
Chevron · “Abril 2026” · chevron, compact-card container, fixed-width tabular label, next disabled at current. Month is one app-level store key in the URL. Range variant for Tendencias. Header grammar everywhere: title left, control right, no subtitles, no wordmark.
<GroupSummary /> T6 →
V2 header: count + aggregate delta (“7 sobre límite · −$486.000”). Mandatory above any 3+ same-state rows; state word once per group; rows carry deltas only. Ladder: V1 34/800 · V2 15/600 · V3 13 · V4 11. Healthy groups collapse behind a Disclosure.
chartTheme T2 →
One recharts theme object: chart-1→brass, chart-2→sage-dark, horizontal hairlines only, no axis lines, dashed 4-3 forecasts at 50%, tabular compact labels, isAnimationActive: false. Two tiers: gauges judge (max one per screen, verdict-bearing), charts explain (never verdict-bearing).
b · qué se elimina
× Dashboard status dot · “SOBRE LIMITE” / “ATENCION” pills · “Te pasaste del presupuesto” strings → <Verdict /> × Page-dim on hero expand · silent growers · bare “Ver” links → <Disclosure /> × “Abr” pill · below-hero selectors · title+subtitle headers · wordmark-as-header → <MonthControl /> + canonical header × Per-row uppercase alarm pills · equal-volume walls → <GroupSummary /> + ladder × Unthemed recharts defaults · per-card chart palettes → chartTheme × Letter-badge account glyphs → colored dot everywhere (the smaller, quieter of the two systems)
c · reglas de copy — cierran la deriva
1Verdict vocabulary is closed: Vas bien · Cerca del límite · Te pasaste · Atención. No synonyms, no uppercase. 2Disclosure labels: “Ver + qué” ↔ “Ocultar + qué”. Never bare “Ver”. 3Selector shows “Abril 2026” only; “abr” on axes; relative dates only in rows. 4Celebration eyebrows: DEUDA SALDADA · MES CERRADO BAJO PLAN · TODO PAGADO. Detail in tuteo, past tense, no exclamation. 5Past months read-only: “Mes cerrado” line, verdicts in past tense (“Cerraste bajo plan”). 6Group summaries: “N sobre límite” / “N en orden” / “N de M pagados”.
d · secuencia — cuatro fases por apalancamiento
Fase 1 · Veredicto T1
Build <Verdict />. Converge dashboard dot y pill de Presupuesto; add the slot to Deudas, Recurrentes, Plan. Delete the three dialects. Resolves finding 1, 8 (parcial). North-star unblocked.
Fase 2 · Marco T5 + T3
Canonical header + shared month store on the five surfaces; replace every expand with <Disclosure />; remove hero dim. Mechanical, low risk, wide surface. Resolves findings 3, 4, 6.
Fase 3 · Densidad T6 + T2
Ladder + <GroupSummary /> on Presupuesto and Deudas; apply chartTheme to Tendencias, forecast, income-expense; unify account glyphs. Resolves findings 2, 5, 7.
Fase 4 · Calidez T4
The three celebration moments (deuda saldada, mes cerrado, todo pagado) + residue rows in Actividad reciente. Ships last: depends on Verdict, ring, and motion tokens. Resolves finding 9. One event, once, brass-on-number.
