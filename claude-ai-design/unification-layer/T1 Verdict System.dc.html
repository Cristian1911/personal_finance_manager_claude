1a Zeta · unification layer · T1

Unified verdict system

One component, one vocabulary, one slot. Every hero answers ¿Voy bien? with the same four Spanish words, color-bound to the semantic tokens that already exist. The verdict is the only element on a screen allowed to speak at full volume about status — everything below it enumerates in compact form. Brass never carries a verdict: brass stays actions and brand.

The slot is fixed: eyebrow → number → verdict → detail → meta, identical on every hero. 8px above the chip, 6px chip→detail, 12px detail→meta. One full-volume verdict per screen; never in brass.

b · vocabulario — cuatro estados, tres tokens
Vas bien --z-income · check Within plan. Debt falling. All recurrentes paid. The state the whole app steers toward — today it is nearly mute (feeds T4).
Cerca del límite --z-alert · arrow-up-right 75–99% of a limit consumed, or pace projects crossing it before month end. A magnitude warning — no action required yet.
Te pasaste --z-debt · circle-x ≥100% of a limit, or a balance that grew this month. The only state that means “the number is bad now” — so it owns the only red.
Atención --z-alert · triangle-alert Your action is needed: a payment due, movements sin categoría, a data gap hiding the real pace. Feeds the “Por resolver” inbox.

Deliberate: Cerca del límite and Atención share --z-alert. Color carries severity; the word and icon carry the reason. No new hues — the mapping is income / alert / debt and nothing else.

d · migración — convergencia de los dialectos existentes

The dashboard’s dot-plus-sentence and Presupuesto’s uppercase pill are both partial verdicts — the dot has color but no word, the pill has a word but no fixed slot and the wrong case. Both converge onto this component without layout re-flow: the dashboard’s 6px dot becomes the “Vas bien” chip in the same position under the hero number, and its existing sentence (“Vas $320.000 bajo el ritmo ideal”) survives verbatim as the verdict’s detail line. Presupuesto’s “SOBRE LIMITE” re-cases to “Te pasaste”, re-binds from its ad-hoc styling to the surface-debt recipe, and — critically — moves from being stamped on every over-limit row to a single hero-slot verdict, with rows demoted to the 11px compact form. Deudas, Recurrentes and Plan, which today render no judgment at all, adopt the full slot as shown above.
