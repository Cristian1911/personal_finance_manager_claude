5a Zeta · unification layer · T5

Shared time model

One header, one clock. Every month-scoped surface renders the identical header row: title left, month control right — and the month is one piece of shared state, not five. Change it anywhere and every tab agrees. Below, the three phones share a single live month; step it on any one of them.

a · el encabezado canónico
Plan Abril 2026
TITLE H1 22px/600 (28px desktop), sentence case, matches the tab name exactly: Inicio, Movimientos, Plan, Deudas, Recurrentes. No subtitles — the eyebrow system inside the cards carries context. The wordmark leaves the headers entirely; it lives on splash and auth only. CONTROL One month stepper, always top-right: chevron · label · chevron in a compact-card container (#111, border white/6, radius 10). Chevrons stroke 1.5, hit areas 32×44. Fixed-width label so stepping never shifts layout. Next-chevron disables at the current month. LABEL “Abril 2026” — full month, capitalized, plus year. Always. Never “Abr”, never “Este mes”, never year-less. tabular-nums so 2026 never wobbles. STATE The month is app-level state (one store key, in the URL). Switching tabs never resets it; stepping it on Plan steps it on Movimientos. Five clocks become one.

Past months are read-only and say so. Stepping back adds one quiet line under the header — “Mes cerrado” — and hides capture actions. The verdict renders in past tense: “Cerraste bajo plan”.
What this deletes: the “Abr” pill above the title, the selector rendered below the hero, the title+subtitle header, and the wordmark-as-header. Four patterns collapse into one slot.
