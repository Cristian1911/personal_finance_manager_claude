2a Zeta · unification layer · T2

Data-viz identity

Two tiers, one language. Gauges judge; charts explain. Tier 1 is the signature moment — the hand-rolled ring, seeded by RITMO, verdict-bearing, at most one per screen. Tier 2 is the workhorse — recharts, analytical, as many as needed, never verdict-bearing. Both share one DNA: ink grid, brass-draws-what-you-did, tabular numbers, zero motion. Tendencias stops reading like a different app the moment it adopts the DNA.

a · shared dna — every zeta chart
FRAME Charts live inside surface cards under an eyebrow. No plot background, no axis lines, no legend boxes, no vertical gridlines. The card is the frame. GRID Horizontal hairlines only, rgba(255,255,255,0.06), max 3. Zero baselines and limits are solid white/12 reference lines. SERIES Brass draws what you did. Sage-dark draws what to compare against — ideal, promedio, proyección target. Income green and expense orange appear only when the two meet in one frame; debt red only for debt. Max 2 series + 1 reference per chart. FORECAST Dashed 4 3, 50% opacity, same hue as its series — never a new color. The only dot allowed is the 3px “hoy” marker. NUMBERS Every label tabular-nums, compact COP format ($3,76M · $178K), 9–10px sage-dark. Label the decision numbers — peak and current — not every point. Keys are inline swatch chips, not legend boxes. MOTION None. isAnimationActive={false} always. Tooltips render as compact cards (#111, border white/6).
