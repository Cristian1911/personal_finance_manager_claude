Your four changes applied:

  • Quick actions are Categoría + Persona/Comercio.
  • The source appears only in the detail, as an icon.
  • Facts at the top, actions at the bottom.
  • Nota is demoted.

The big cut: what used to be six tiles (Dividir, Préstamo, Pagó…, A mi cuenta, Reembolso, Me pagaron) is now one question, "Qué fue", because they're mutually exclusive answers.

2 · The detail (⋯): three options

All three start with the same identity: amount, name, a line of facts with the source as an icon (✉ email · 🔔 notification · ✎ by hand · 📄 PDF), and the raw bank text when there is one. The difference is how they ask for the rest.

3 · Which actions survive

Critical pass over the 14 previous actions plus v1's. Rule: each one has to answer a question the person actually asks themselves about a movement.

ActionVerdictWhy / where it lives
CategoríaKeepThe most common fix. Open row + detail. After picking: "¿Siempre para Rappi?" (only if it's a merchant).
Comercio / PersonaKeep"Who it was with". Renaming a merchant and assigning a person use the same control. Choosing a person opens "¿Qué fue con Ana?".
Dividir, Préstamo, Pagó un pago fijo, A mi cuenta, Reembolso, Me pagaronMerge → "Qué fue"They're mutually exclusive answers about the kind of money. One control with options that depend on direction (gasto / entrada). Six tiles become one row.
CuotasContextualOnly on card purchases. It appears in the list as "Cuotas · 1" only then. Revisar already asks it for purchases ≥ $300.000.
NotaKeep, demotedLast row (A) or a link (B). Search finds it.
Ignorar ("No es un movimiento")Keep, quietA text link above Listo. The wording explains itself better than "Ignorar".
EliminarMerge into the same linkOn a manual entry, "No es un movimiento" deletes it, with Deshacer for 5 s. On a bank movement it ignores it. One button, the right effect each time.
Fix amount / date / accountManual entries onlyTapping the amount or the facts line of a manual entry edits them. Bank movements aren't editable (the bank wins), so there's no locked "Del banco" row to show.
Cuenta para Disponible (account toggle)Cut from hereIt's a setting of the account, not of a movement; it lives in Mis cuentas. Here it only shows as a status pill when it doesn't count ("No cuenta · va a la factura").
Hacer recurrente (v1)Cutv2 detects recurring payments and Revisar suggests them. "Pago fijo" under "Qué fue" covers linking one by hand.
Etiquetas (v1)CutTags are frozen in v2 (S2-4).
Editar datos / Editar todo (v1)CutTwo edit modes; v2 has no edit modes. Replaced by direct tapping on manual entries.

To decide

1. The detail: A, B or C? I recommend A · Lista. It's the one closest to what worked in v1, the actions sit at the bottom within thumb reach, and it doesn't change shape between purchases and income.
2. Merging Eliminar into "No es un movimiento"? (it deletes manual entries and ignores bank ones). Or keep two separate links.
3. Moving the "Cuenta para Disponible" toggle out of the detail (it's already in #445) and leaving only the status pill.
