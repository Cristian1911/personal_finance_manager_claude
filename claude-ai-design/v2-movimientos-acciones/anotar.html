Anotar: the center "+"

For what Zeta doesn't capture on its own: cash, an income, money between your own accounts, something from the past. Goal: the common case in two taps. Amount first (the onboarding pad), then who (recent destinatarios, with the comercio/persona shapes), then Guardar. Everything else is already prefilled and gets changed only if needed.

As you type the amount, Anotar shows how it leaves your Disponible ("Te quedan $376.100 hasta el 30"). It's the question you ask yourself before spending, answered in the same gesture.

1 · Gasto (opens like this)

The amount takes focus straight away. Recientes are your usual destinatarios: tapping one fills it in, along with its category and usual account. Cuenta and Fecha come prefilled (last cash account, today). Guardar lights up as soon as there's an amount.

2 · Dictar (the mic on the pad)

"Almuerzo 45 mil en efectivo" fills amount, what and account. It runs on the phone with the offline parser (S4-4); if it doesn't understand, it leaves the text in "En qué" and you complete the rest. The Inicio mic leads here.

What's in and what isn't

PieceDecisionWhy
Amount + pad firstInIt's the only thing Zeta can't guess. The onboarding pad, already designed.
Impact on Disponible while typingInZeta's main question, answered before spending ("¿Me alcanza?" / Flow 07 in v1, built in).
Recientes (destinatarios)InOne tap fills who, category and account.
CategoríaIn from M3Until there are categories on the phone, it doesn't show.
Cuenta, FechaIn, prefilledChanged only when needed (C13, something from the past).
DictarIn (mic on the pad)C5. Offline parser; the Inicio mic leads here.
Compartido, préstamo, cuotasOut of AnotarThat's "Qué fue" in the movement's Detalle, after saving. Anotar stays short.
Nota, pago recurrente, suscripción (v1)OutNota in Detalle. Pagos fijos are created in Pagos or from the destinatario's sheet.
Confirmation screenOutThe number moving + Deshacer (5 s) confirm it.

By milestone

M1 (the number): Gasto and Ingreso with amount, en qué, cuenta, fecha, Guardar + Deshacer, and the impact on Disponible. That's what M1's acceptance asks for ("a manual expense moves Disponible"). M2: Entre cuentas (pairing transfers). M3: Categoría and Recientes by destinatario (they need destinatarios and categories synced). Dictar: the offline parser is ready to bring in when capture arrives (M5).

To decide

1. The impact line ("Te quedan $376.100 hasta el 30") while you type. Does it stay? It's the most "Zeta" part of the screen, but it's one more line.
2. Three types on top (Gasto · Ingreso · Entre cuentas), or only Gasto with "Ingreso" as a smaller switch? Spending is 90% of what gets written down by hand.
3. Without Recientes in M1 (there are no destinatarios on the phone yet), M1 shows a simple "En qué" field. Fine, or should destinatarios move up to M1?
