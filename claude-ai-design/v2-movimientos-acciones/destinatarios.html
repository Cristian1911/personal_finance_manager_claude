Destinatarios: comercio and persona

One system (decision S8-2). The who of a movement is a destinatario: a comercio or a persona, with the same patterns, category and sheet. What kind of money it was (compartido, préstamo…) is the separate "Qué fue" question.

RComercio: rounded square + store
LGPersona: circle with an outline + person, two initials

Shape does the work, without color (color = state, S5-R). The glyph backs it up at small sizes and for people who don't notice the shape. A persona shows two initials (like a contact); a comercio shows one.

1 · In Movimientos you can already tell them apart

The movement's avatar is its destinatario's avatar. A transfer to Laura looks like a person; Rappi looks like a store. A movement without a destinatario uses the generic square with the first letter of the bank text.

How it works underneath (no new tables)

  • Same model as v1: destinatarios.kind (comercio | persona), destinatario_rules (patterns, priority, first match wins), default_category_id, transactions.destinatario_id. "Lo reconozco por" lists the rules with their match_count.
  • Debts: personal_debts.destinatario_id already points to the person. The "Te debe" block reads from there, so it's the same number as Te deben.
  • Policy (Siempre / Casi siempre / Pregúntame) is merchant_policy from the plan, extended to people. Default: comercio → Siempre once you choose a category; persona → Pregúntame.
  • Pagos fijos (plural): a destinatario can have several (v1 already supports this since the subscriptions work: N templates per merchant). Each template carries destinatario_id. A charge is linked by destinatario + amount (±tolerance) + day; if two of its pagos fijos fit the same charge, Revisar asks which one. Detalle › Qué fue › "Pago fijo" lists only that destinatario's. Each row opens the same "Detalle del pago" as Pagos.
  • Changing the kind ("Es una persona") keeps its patterns and movements; it only changes the shape, the default policy and whether the debt block appears.
  • v2 commands: setDestinatario (on a movement, with "siempre" → adds a rule and applies it to the past ones), createDestinatario, renameDestinatario, mergeDestinatarios, setDestinatarioPolicy, addDestinatarioRule / removeDestinatarioRule. These replace the plan's renameMerchant/mergeMerchants.

To decide

1. The shapes: squircle + store for comercio, outlined circle + person for persona, with 1 vs 2 initials. Does it work for you, or would you rather have a real photo/logo later (for example a contact's picture)?
2. Whether a person gets a category: I propose an optional one (e.g. Familia) with "Pregúntame" by default. The alternative is that money with people never has a category and is grouped as "Personas".
3. Who can be a destinatario: beyond comercios and personas, are there others? E.g. "Arrendador", "Colegio", "EPS". I'd treat them as comercios (a company or institution) and keep only two kinds.
