Mis cuentas · Cuenta · ver sus movimientos

Builds on the "Mis cuentas" screen in Claude Design's Z Cuentas, plus what the map showed was missing: cards and loans, getting into one account, its movements, and Cuadrar. Every screen carries the new bar: 4 tabs + a center "+" that opens Anotar.

Ways in to Mis cuentas: the Mis cuentas widget on Inicio · Ajustes › Mis cuentas · the Disponible detail ("¿De dónde sale?") · tapping the account inside a movement's Detalle.

1 · Mis cuentas

On top, what counts for your Disponible. Cuentas and Tarjetas/créditos stay apart, because their numbers mean different things (money you have vs money you owe). Each account's switch is the Claude Design one. Turning one off asks first.

4 · Agregar

One button, four kinds. Each asks only what that kind needs: account → name + balance today; card → limit, cut and due day; loan → balance, cuota, day. Plus the shortcut "Desde un extracto" (A11), which creates it from the PDF.

Navigation bar: 4 tabs + "+"

Inicio · Movimientos · + · Pagos · Revisar (with a counter). The "+" is black (black = tap), sticks out of the bar like the v1 FAB and opens Anotar, with Dictar inside. Ajustes is reached from the avatar on Inicio. Mis cuentas, Cuenta and the destinatario sheet open within the tab you came from, so the bar stays visible and you can go back.

To decide

1. Cards and loans in Mis cuentas, or only in Pagos › Deudas? I recommend both: Mis cuentas is "everything I have and owe", Pagos is "what I have to pay". Tapping a card here opens the same card screen as Pagos.
2. A switch on each row of the list (as Claude Design drew it), or only inside the account? A row switch is fast, but it's easy to turn off by accident. With the confirmation it's safe.
3. "Saldo" with its origin ("con lo último de hoy 12:41"): it says the balance is calculated from the last known balance plus the movements since. Clear enough, or "≈" when it isn't confirmed by the bank?
