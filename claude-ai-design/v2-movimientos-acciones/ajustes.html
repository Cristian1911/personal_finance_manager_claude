Ajustes

Reached from the avatar on Inicio (S8-1). It builds on Claude Design's Z Ajustes, reorganized by what you want to change, plus what the use cases need that it didn't have: Cuándo me pagan and Ahorro (the inputs to your number), Destinatarios (S8-2), Bloqueo (K9), your profile and signing out. Plantillas and Ignorados (rare) go inside Fuentes. Apariencia, Tu plan and Privacidad stay as Claude Design drew them.
