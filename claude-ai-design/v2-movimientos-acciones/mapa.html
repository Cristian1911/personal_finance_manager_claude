3 · Destinatarios: comercio and persona, one system

What already exists (v1, same database)

  • destinatarios with kind (comercio | persona), name, default category, notes.
  • destinatario_rules: several patterns per destinatario (contains / exact, priority, the first match wins) and how many times it matched.
  • transactions.destinatario_id saved per movement. Retroactive applying, suggestions for movements that repeat.
  • personal_debts.destinatario_id: debts already point to a person-destinatario.
  • Recurring templates carry destinatario_id: the payment matcher anchors on destinatario + account.

What v2 was missing

  • The v2 plan says "merchant" everywhere ("Hoja de comercio", renameMerchant, mergeMerchants, merchant_policy). People were never designed as destinatarios.
  • So "Persona" got mixed up with debts. It isn't the same thing: sending money to my sister can be a gift, a payment for something, a loan or my share of something shared.
  • Correction: the open row's quick action is Destinatario (comercio or persona). What kind of money it was (compartido, préstamo…) is the separate "Qué fue" question.

How the destinatario works in v2 (proposal)

MomentComercio (Rappi)Persona (Laura, my sister)
Recognize itPattern on the bank text: RAPPI*Pattern on the transfer: NEQUI A LAURA GIRALDO, TRANSF LAURA G, a phone number
First timeHaiku names it ("Rappi") if online; otherwise the bank text. Revisar: "¿Es Rappi?"Revisar: "¿Quién es Laura Giraldo?" → new person or one you already have
From then onDefault category + policy (Siempre / Casi siempre / Pregúntame)Optional default category (e.g. "Familia"). Policy: Pregúntame by default, since money with people varies
PatternsIts payments repeat → suggests a Pago fijo (Netflix)Its payments repeat → also Pago fijo (the monthly payment to your mom); its repayments → "¿Es lo que te debía?"

One sheet, one model, one picker. Kind changes two things only: whether the debt section appears, and the default policy. A person can't become a comercio by accident: the picker creates with the kind of the tab you're on (the v1 bug from PR #282).

The destinatario picker (row and Detalle)

Opens with search + two tabs Comercios · Personas, already on the tab matching the bank text (a transfer → Personas). At the top, the suggestion Zeta recognized. "+ Nuevo" creates it with the current tab's kind and saves the bank text as its first pattern. After you choose, one optional question: "¿Las de NEQUI A LAURA G son siempre Laura?" (Sí → pattern; past movements too).

5 · Gaps found

  1. Accounts. Mis cuentas and Cuenta aren't designed, even though five use cases (A8, A13, B5, F1, F5) and D14 depend on them. Neither is "see the movements of an account".
  2. Destinatarios. They were only "comercio", and there's no list screen. Persona = destinatario, and debts already point to it.
  3. Writing things down. v2 has no screen for jotting down an expense, even though M1's acceptance is "a manual expense moves Disponible". Today it goes to v1.
  4. Tabs. The plan (3) and the design (5) disagree. Today Movimientos is only reachable from widgets.
  5. Ajustes. No v2 screen. Everything that lives "in Ajustes" (Cuándo me pagan, Ahorro, Fuentes…) has no way in yet.
  6. Cuadrar saldo and Undo. Planned, but with no designed way in or toast.
