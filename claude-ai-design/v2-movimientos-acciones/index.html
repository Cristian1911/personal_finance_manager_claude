Qué puedes hacer con un movimiento

The current Zeta's pattern carried into v2. A tap opens the row in place with the three most common actions. Más opens the full sheet, where every action has a fixed slot: it changes state where it sits and never moves or disappears.

Three levels

1. Row (closed). Name, amount, then the category (once M3 arrives) or a "No cuenta / Ignorado" chip, the time and the account.
2. Row open (tap). One row open at a time, and the rest of the list dims. It shows where the movement came from and the trio Categoría · Dividir · Más. Category and splitting are what people do most right after spending. A slot with a value shows that value ("Domicilios", "Con Ana").
3. Más → Detalle sheet. Everything else, in three blocks:

  • Qué fue: a 2×3 grid of fixed slots.
  • Corregir: rows for fixing data.
  • Al pie: Ignorar / Eliminar.
A purchase and an income have different grids, but within each type the slots never move.
